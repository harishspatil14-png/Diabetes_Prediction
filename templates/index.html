WHAT GOES INTO AN ESTIMATE

Clinical measurements

Use recent values from your health records. The model considers all eight measurements together.

  • PregnanciesNumber of pregnancies
  • GlucoseGlucose concentration (mg/dL)
  • Blood pressureDiastolic pressure (mmHg)
  • Skin thicknessTriceps skin fold (mm)
  • InsulinSerum insulin (μU/mL)
  • Body mass indexBMI (kg/m²)
  • Diabetes pedigreeFamily-history index
